Step-by-Step

Installation Guide

Ensure your bootloader is unlocked and you have the recommended custom recovery (TWRP, OrangeFox, or PBRP) flashed.

01

Backup Everything

Backup your internal storage, photos, and OTP authenticators to a PC or cloud drive. A clean flash requires formatting data.

02

Reboot to Recovery

Boot into OrangeFox or TWRP recovery. Wipe System, Dalvik-Cache, and format data (type yes).

03

Flash Firmware & ROM

Flash the recommended vendor/firmware if required by your maintainer, followed by the OctaviOS.zip package.

04

Reboot & Enjoy

Reboot system. First boot may take 2-4 minutes while ART pre-compiles. Welcome to OctaviOS!

Dirty Flash note

Dirty flashing between versions is generally supported if upgrading official builds. Clean flash is strictly mandatory when switching from other ROMs.
